Where we are

We're based in the Beaches area of Toronto. Boards are made and tested locally, and pickup is arranged by message — no shipping, no waiting on a courier.

Local pickup only. We hand boards over in person so you can see them running before you commit. Message us to arrange a time and a spot.

Good reasons to get in touch

  • Buying a board — availability, what comes in the box, or a demo before you decide.
  • Setting one up — the getting-started guide covers the Wi-Fi setup, team picker, and modes, but if you get stuck we'll walk you through it on the phone.
  • MLB Scoreboard or Baseball MiniBoard — both are in progress. Ask to be told when they're ready.
  • Something not working — a board that won't join your Wi-Fi, a display that needs a settings change, or a question about firmware updates.
