Where we are

We're based in the Beaches area of Toronto. Boards are made and tested locally. We ship anywhere in Canada, and if you're nearby, local pickup is always an option.

Shipping or pickup — your call. We ship across Canada, and you can also pick up in the Beaches area if you'd rather see the board running before you commit. Message us and we'll work out whichever is easier.

Good reasons to get in touch

  • Buying a board — availability, what comes in the box, shipping, or a demo before you decide.
  • Setting one up — the getting-started guide covers the Wi-Fi setup, team picker, and modes, but if you get stuck we'll walk you through it on the phone.
  • MLB Scoreboard or Baseball MiniBoard — both are in progress. Ask to be told when they're ready.
  • Something not working — a board that won't join your Wi-Fi, a display that needs a settings change, or a question about firmware updates.
