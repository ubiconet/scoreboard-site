How it works

  1. Plug it in

    USB-C, about 30 seconds of boot. It works cordless for around 3 hours, or indefinitely on the cable.

  2. Join the Wi-Fi, pick your teams

    One page on your phone. Choose your network, choose up to three teams, save. No app, no account.

  3. It does the rest

    Live score, period, clock, shots and penalties — updating every five seconds. Goal horn when your team scores. Standings, headlines and trivia the rest of the time.
