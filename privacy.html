Privacy

The MiniBoard app talks to your own scoreboard, on your own network. That is the whole of it.

The app collects nothing

The Sports MiniBoards Android app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not collect personal data, and it does not share any with us or with anyone else. The list of boards you have set up is stored on your phone only.

The app talks only to your board

The app connects to your scoreboard over your own Wi-Fi, using the address your board gives it. It never contacts our servers, because we do not run any. Nothing about you, your board or your network is sent anywhere.

What the board itself fetches

A MiniBoard fetches live game data from the league's own public feed so it can show scores. That request carries no information about you, your board or your network — it is the same request any viewer of that feed would make.

The one permission the app asks for

To list the name of a scoreboard's setup network so you know which one to join, the app asks Android for permission to see nearby Wi-Fi networks. Android will not show network names to an app without it.

That permission is used for one purpose: reading the name of a nearby network so it can tell you “your scoreboard's network is called NHL_SCOREBOARD”. The app does not record your location, does not send it anywhere, and does not use it for anything else. Finding and controlling a board does not need it, so you can decline and the app still works.

What we keep

Nothing. We do not operate a server that the app or the board connects to, so there is no account, no usage record and no cookie on this page beyond what any web server logs. If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply to you.

Children

The app and the boards are not directed at children under 13, and they collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes with it, and the app's store listing will say so too.
