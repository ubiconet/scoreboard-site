Returns

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days of delivery. The buyer is responsible for return shipping costs, and for any loss of value if the item isn't returned in original condition.

If a board arrives damaged or isn't working, email us first — we'll make it right rather than making you post it back. A board that's been running for months and something has gone wrong with it is a different situation from something that was faulty on arrival, and we'd rather sort that out directly.

Warranty The board updates its own firmware over your Wi-Fi, and a lot of what looks like a fault is fixed by a firmware update. If something isn't right, get in touch before sending anything back and we'll walk you through it.

Custom colourways

Custom two-colour finishes are made to order for an extra $15, and they're not returnable once they've been made to your team's colours. Ask before you order and we'll send you a picture of what it will look like.

Payment

We accept Etsy Payments for orders placed on Etsy. For direct orders, email us and we'll send you an invoice you can pay by e-transfer. We don't take card numbers over email or in the chat.
