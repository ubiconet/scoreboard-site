Getting started

From box to live NHL scores in about a minute.

  1. Power up

    Plug the USB-C cable into the back of the board. Plug the other end into any 5 V / 2 A source — a phone charger, a USB battery pack, or a computer. The color screen lights up with a startup splash, then settles on the NHL_SCOREBOARD setup screen.

    Battery option: leave it plugged in to charge for an hour or two before the first time you take it cordless. A full charge runs the board roughly 3 hours.

  2. Connect your phone to the board's Wi-Fi

    The board broadcasts its own Wi-Fi network for setup: NHL_SCOREBOARD. On your phone, open Settings → Wi-Fi, pick NHL_SCOREBOARD, and connect. The network is open by default — no password — so don't leave this on a public network.

    If the captive portal doesn't auto-open: open any browser on your phone and navigate to 192.168.4.1 (the board's setup page). On Android, signing in opens it. On iOS, the same.

  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi and your teams

    The captive portal shows a single form: Wi-Fi network name, password, and three team priority dropdowns. Pick your home Wi-Fi from the dropdown (or type it manually for hidden networks), enter the password, and select up to three teams in priority order — Priority 1 is the one that gets the color screen.

    Tap Save & Connect. The board reboots (~30 seconds) and joins your home Wi-Fi.

    Don't change Wi-Fi? leave the Wi-Fi fields alone and just pick teams. Saving a team-only change doesn't drop the connection.

  4. Wait for first sync

    The board shows an idle clock on the matrices while it talks to the NHL feed (~30–60 seconds). Once your team's next game is within the lead time, the color screen flips to the live game and the matrices carry the score.

    You're done. The board runs itself from here.

Modes

The board has a few operating modes you can switch between from the captive portal. Pick the one that fits what you're doing.

Manual Mode

Full manual control of the display. Useful for a kid's game, a charity match, or any sport where you're calling your own score from the bench. From a dedicated page you can set the clock countdown, scores, period, which penalty LEDs are lit, and per-team shots.

Trivia Mode

Hockey trivia between games. The board poses questions, you answer from a phone on the same network. Hundreds of built-in questions, multiple-choice, with team logos for the answer keys. The color screen runs the game; the matrices carry the score.

Troubleshooting

  • No live game showing up. Check that you've picked teams in the setup portal (Priority 1, 2, 3) and that the next game is within ~24 hours. The board polls the schedule once a minute; updates can take up to 60 s to land.
  • Setup portal won't open. Some phones cache the captive-portal intent. After connecting to NHL_SCOREBOARD, open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1 directly.
  • Score hasn't changed in a while. The board pulls a fresh feed every 5 seconds while a game is live; if it's been flat for 5+ minutes and the game is supposed to be going, you might be looking at an intermission. Check the period matrix — it's blank or showing a dash during intermissions.
  • Goal horn is too loud / too quiet. Hardware volume on the amp is set at build time. If you want it changed, drop a note when you pick the board up.
