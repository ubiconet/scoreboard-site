What you see

  • Score digitsTwo large 8×8 LED matrices — home and away — readable across the room
  • Period indicatorThird 8×8 matrix shows 1, 2, 3, OT, or SO
  • Game clockBright 4-digit display
  • Penalty lightsFour LEDs — two for each team — light up while a team is short-handed. The color screen shows who has the penalties and how much time remains.
  • Goal hornSounds when your team scores!

No Game Today?

It rotates upcoming games with a countdown to puck drop, shows your division standings, and scrolls the latest NHL headlines. Period timer shows the current time.

Setup is 60 seconds

  1. Plug it in (or run on battery).
  2. On your phone, join the NHL_SCOREBOARD Wi-Fi network it broadcasts.
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi, pick your teams, save.
  4. Done. It reboots into live mode (or standby, depending on the schedule).

No app to install. No account to create. No subscription.
